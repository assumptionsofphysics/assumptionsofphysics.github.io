Status and funding goals

Why fund us. The Assumptions of Physics research program aims to build a more solid formal and conceptual foundation for physics, in line with the foundations of mathematics and computer science. As this necessarily bridges topics across mathematics, physics and philosophy of science, standard academic funding mechanisms are not set up to support this type of research. To see how we plan to use our funds, see the funding needs table

Status. To see what has been done and what remains to be done, see the Reverse Physics status table and the Physical Mathematics status table.

Reverse Physics status

The core conditions and assumptions of classical mechanics have been identified and are available. Next step is to increase the level of rigor to the new standards being developed.

  • Classical Hamiltonian mechanics - assumptions and reconstruction
  • Classical Lagrangian mechanics - assumptions and reconstruction
  • Classical Newtonian mechanics - assumptions and reconstruction
  • Principle of least action - physics and geometry
  • Phase space - assumptions and reconstruction
  • Extending variational principles to non-conservative dynamics
  • Generalize classical uncertainty principle to non-canonical variables

The core conditions and assumptions of quantum mechanics have been identified. We are finalizing the ideas and finding the best way to present the results.

  • Classical limit - assumptions and reconstruction
  • Quantum state space - assumptions and reconstruction
  • Time evolution - assumptions and reconstruction
  • Projective measurements - assumptions and reconstruction
  • Path integral formulation - physics and geometry
  • Difference between classical and quantum Lie algebra

Preliminary ideas to recover thermodynamic entropy have been identified. They need to be tested and developed into a consistent theory.

  • Entropy as count of evolutions
  • Equilibria and equation of state

To generalize results from classical mechanics to classical field theory, a suitable theory of information theory, symplectic geometry and probability needs to be developed.

  • Classical electromagnetism
  • Principle of stationary action for field theories
  • General relativity

Work on quantum field theories is planned only when the work on classical field theory and quantum mechanics is mature enough.

  • Quantum field theories
  • Specific quantum field theories: QED, QCD, Weak theory
  • Path integral formulation for field theories

Physical Mathematics status

The core requirements for experimental verifiability of a scientific theory have been identified and formalized. A precise mathematical theory is built on top, which recovers standard topological and σ-algebraic tools. A few areas for improvement still exist.

  • Principle of scientific objectivity as basis for logic of experimental verifiability
  • Logic of experimental verifiability leads to topologies (set of verifiable statements) and σ-algebras (statements associated with experimental tests)
  • Recovering well-behaved functions in physics as consistency with experimental verifiability (i.e. topological continuity)
  • Precise link to frames/locales and category theory
  • Characterization of open maps and compact spaces in terms of experimental verifiability

The core requirements for experimental reproducibility in a scientific theory have been identified and are being formalized. A precise mathematical theory is being built to recover topological vector spaces, information theory, probability theory and other structures. Key problems still need to be understood and addressed.

  • Principle of scientific reproducibility as basis for ensemble spaces and their requirements
  • Statistical mixing leads to convex structure on the ensemble space
  • Basic axiomatization of entropy leading to recovery of Shannon entropy and other general purpose tools
  • Definition of general purpose non-additive measures that recover additivity in classical spaces
  • Definition of random variables that generalize the classical and quantum cases
  • Various problems, such as completeness of the topological convex space, topological embedding into TVS for infinite dimensions, ...

Preliminary ideas to recover some of the other mathematical structures from physical requirements.

  • Statement granularity to recover physical dimensions and measure theory
  • A generalized notion of differentiability built on TVS that can recover different standard notions in different cases
